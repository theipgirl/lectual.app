# The life of a trademark client at RPB Law Firm

2026-11-23

**What this is.** One account of a trademark client's whole life at the firm, from first hearing of us to a registered mark and its renewals, with every way out.
**What it is built from.** The three written SOPs (intake, pre-filing, post-filing), the firm's email and text templates, the team's recorded meetings from Aug 19 to Nov 17, and a read of what the Lectual app does today.
**How to read it.** Each step shows what the SOP says ("As written") and what people said in meetings they actually do ("As practiced"), kept apart on purpose. Every line ends with its source in brackets.
**What it is not.** It is not a new SOP, not legal guidance, and not a decision. Nothing was changed in any system to write it.
**Where it is thin.** The post-filing walkthrough was never recorded, and parts of the intake SOP could not be read. Those places are marked.

Source labels: (SOP intake), (SOP pre-filing), (SOP post-filing), (SOP agents), (templates) for the email handbook, (meeting, date) for a recorded meeting, (Lectual) for the app. "Not stated" means the sources are silent. The SOP agents document was found but not read, so no line below cites it (templates).

Gap labels used throughout:
- **No written procedure**: the step happens, but no document says how.
- **Not in Lectual yet**: the step relies on Lawmatics, a spreadsheet or someone's memory, and Lectual does not do it ("partly" where it partly does).
- **Sources disagree**: documents or people say different things.
- **Not on record**: something the research could not see. This is not a gap in the firm's process.

### Two numbering traps

- **"Phase 8" means two different things.** The intake SOP calls long-term nurture "Phase 8" (SOP intake). The pre-filing SOP uses "8. Questionnaire Complete" (SOP pre-filing). Caitlyn noted the clash herself (meeting, Nov 12), and the pre-filing SOP asks to rename intake nurture (SOP pre-filing, D7). In this document, nurture is called "long-term nurture" and phase 8 means Questionnaire Complete.
- **There is no phase 17.** The first pre-filing draft ran stages 8 to 17. Version 0.4 merged search and opinion letter into one stage and renumbered the rest, so the old 17 became today's "16. Application Filed" (SOP pre-filing, revision history; templates). The post-filing SOP starts at 18 (SOP post-filing). Separately, Lectual's docket codes put "Application Filed" at 17 (Lectual); see "Where the sources disagree".

**Who owns it.** Taylor by default; Amor for capture and nurture; Rebecca for the session and every approval; Dawn for payment (SOP intake).

- **Starts when:** a person meets the firm through the website, social media, an event, an activation or a referral (SOP intake).
- **Who:** not stated for the phase as a whole; Taylor builds activation questionnaires (SOP intake).
- **As written:** every lead should become a Lawmatics contact with a source tag; website and QR-code leads arrive by form; cards and referrals are typed in within 1 business day (SOP intake).
