# Recap: automatic consult prep (Nov 24)

## What we decided
- When a lead lands in Consultation Scheduled, Lectual does the full prep with no questions asked and emails it to Rebecca, with a copy to info@. The brief also shows on the client's page in Lectual.
- The email goes three times: when the consult is booked, 24 hours before (only if it was booked more than a day ahead), and 1 hour before. If the consult is moved or cancelled, the emails still waiting to go out are cancelled.
- The prep reads every place the client's answers live:
  - the Lectual intake and assessment answers;
  - the intake answers in Lawmatics;
  - the files uploaded on the lead.
- It ships in two steps:
  - S1: the automatic trigger, the facts brief, and the new panel.
  - S2: the full judgment (classes, risk read, package, USPTO search and the packages PDF).
- Lectual becomes the only consult brief. The older Lawmatics brief, which Rebecca isn't getting, is switched off when S1 goes live.
- The email to the client stays a draft in Approvals. Nothing goes to a client automatically.
